New Student Registration Information System Website

A website-based new student registration information system designed to simplify registration for SMPN 2 Padang Gelugur and provide easy access to information on the registration timeline and other activities.

Background

SMPN 2 Padang Gelugur, a junior high school in Padang Gelugur District, Pasaman Regency, conducts a new student admission (PPDB) program annually. The current conventional PPDB implementation at SMPN 2 Padang Gelugur, involving direct registration at the school and the use of physical forms, creates various obstacles. These issues include the school's considerable distance from the main road, incomplete prospective student files, and ineffective information dissemination. This situation hinders the efficiency of the registration and data verification processes, causing difficulties for both the school and prospective students. To address these challenges, this study focuses on designing a solution to create a website-based PPDB information system that is more efficient and easily accessible.

Problem Statement

  1. Limitations of Conventional and Paper-Based Processes: The New Student Admissions process (Penerimaan Peserta Didik Baru / PPDB) at SMPN 2 Padang Gelugur is currently conducted using conventional methods. Applicants are required to fill out printed forms and travel to the school in person to submit the required documents and complete the re-registration process. Consequently, the data collection process becomes inefficient and time-consuming.
  2. Lack of Transparency and Real-Time Application Tracking: Due to the reliance on a manual system, prospective students are unable to track their application status in real-time and only receive updates once the final admission results are officially announced.
  3. Geographical and Accessibility Constraints: The geographical location of SMPN 2 Padang Gelugur presents a significant obstacle for applicants and parents. Situated far from the main road with limited transportation access—only reachable via motorized pedicabs (becak motor) or private vehicles—the physical commute poses a burden during the admission period.
  4. Disparity in Technology Adoption Across Educational Levels: While the implementation of online PPDB systems has been widely adopted in senior high schools (SMA/SMK) and major urban centers like Padang, its utilization at the junior high school (SMP) level in regencies—specifically Pasaman Regency—remains minimal. As a result, the school has yet to optimize the efficiency and effectiveness of its digital administrative services.

Research

The application design was developed using the Design Thinking methodology, which I consider effective for addressing the challenges of creating an application from scratch. The initial step involved identifying the target users through the "Empathize" phase; an empathy map was created to illustrate the users' perspectives on the existing issues, with the map's content derived from user interviews—specifically with the parents or guardians of students enrolling in the school.

User Persona

The user persona illustrates the needs, goals, and frustrations of parents during the school registration process. It helps identify key motivations and expectations so the design can be tailored for easier navigation, clearer registration steps, and better communication.
